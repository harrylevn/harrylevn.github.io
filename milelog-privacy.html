Privacy Policy

MileLog – Mileage Tracker

Developed by Color Mobile

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

1. Summary

MileLog records the distance of the trips you choose to record and turns them into a mileage log. Your trips are stored only on your device. We have no account system and no server that receives your trips.

2. Location

  • MileLog uses your device's precise location only while a trip is recording — from the moment you tap Start trip until you tap End trip — to measure the distance and draw the route.
  • While a trip is recording, location keeps working when the screen is off or another app is open, so the distance is complete. On Android a notification is shown for the whole recording; on iOS the system shows the location indicator.
  • The route, start and end places are saved on your device only. Street names for the start and end are looked up with the operating system's built-in geocoding service (Apple or Google), which receives those two coordinates.
  • MileLog never sends your trips or routes to us, and never shares them with advertisers.

3. Data stored on your device

Trips (date, time, distance, route, places, category, purpose, vehicle, parking, tolls, notes) and your settings (country, unit, name for reports, vehicles). You can edit or delete any trip, delete everything from Settings → Delete all trips, or uninstall the app.

When you export a CSV or PDF report, the file is created on your device and shared only where you send it.

4. Advertising

MileLog is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your trips, routes or location on any server.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or payment information.
  • We do not sell personal information.

6. Children

MileLog is a tool for drivers and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Tax estimates

Deductions shown in MileLog are estimates based on published IRS, CRA and HMRC rates. They are not tax advice.
