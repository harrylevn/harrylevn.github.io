Sky Today Community Rules

Sky Today: Star Map & Moon

Developed by Color Mobile

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

1. What the community is

The Community tab in Sky Today lets people share photos of the sky that were taken or made inside the app (camera photos with star labels, and star-map pictures), and like and comment on them. Anyone can look at photos. Posting, liking, commenting and reporting need Sign in with Apple or Google. By posting you agree to these rules and to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

2. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not post or comment:

  • nudity, sexual content, violence, gore or anything harmful to minors;
  • hate speech, harassment, threats or bullying;
  • spam, advertising, links, phone numbers or other contact details;
  • photos that are not of the sky, or that you did not take or make in Sky Today;
  • personal information about other people.

Content that breaks these rules is removed and the account that posted it is banned.

3. How we keep it clean

  • Every photo is checked automatically (Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch) before anyone else can see it. Borderline photos wait for a person to review them.
  • Captions and comments with offensive words, links or contact details are blocked.
  • Anyone signed in can report a photo or comment from its menu. A photo reported by several people is hidden at once, and we review every report within 24 hours.
  • You can block any person from the same menu. Their photos and comments disappear for you immediately.

4. What is shared when you post

Your display name, the photo, your caption, the name of your city (never your exact location), the time and the sky event it belongs to. The photo is re-encoded, which removes all camera and GPS metadata. Likes and comments show your display name.

5. Your content and your account

You keep the rights to your photos. By posting you allow us to show them in Sky Today and to resize them for that purpose. You can delete any of your photos or comments at any time. Delete account in the Community tab's account menu permanently deletes your photos, likes, comments and community profile, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple or Google access.

6. Changes

We may update these rules. The date at the top shows the latest version.
